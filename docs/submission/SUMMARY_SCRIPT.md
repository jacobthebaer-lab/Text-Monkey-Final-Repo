# Current presentation script

Target: approximately 80–90 seconds at a measured speaking pace. Rehearse with the final demo; do not claim an unrecorded step succeeded.

Church volunteer coordinators spend a substantial part of their week finding replacements. We heard that directly from coordinators at Cornerstone Church in Boulder. When someone cancels, they need to remember who is available, who is qualified, and who they asked last time.

Text Monkey gives volunteers an ordinary text conversation and gives coordinators one place to manage the resulting work.

Gloo interprets availability and cancellations. Application code checks consent, qualifications, scheduling limits and care holds. Our specialised algorithm ranks eligible replacements using acceptance rate, average response time and time since their last request. Gloo composes a personal invitation for the selected group.

The coordinator reviews the exact message and proposed official changes. The agent cannot approve its own work. Replies resume the workflow, and the first eligible acceptance fills the vacancy without creating a duplicate assignment.

When something is unclear, sensitive or blocked, the coordinator gets control. Text Monkey does not counsel or make pastoral judgments.

Our build document includes the actual prompts, earlier failures and auditable sessions. We distinguish scripted tests, real model calls and delivery evidence. Production texting is planned through registered Twilio numbers.

The goal is less time chasing schedules and more time relating to the people who serve.

## Recording sequence

Show the current coordinator dashboard, a fictional cancellation, eligibility and the reserved batch, exact approval, the reply and proposed assignment, then the audit and one blocked case. Use the current working application's actual states. Capture no private contacts or conversations. Keep the final encoded file at or below 90 seconds and inspect full playback.
