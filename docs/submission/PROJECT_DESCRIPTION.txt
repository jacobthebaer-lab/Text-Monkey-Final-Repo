Churches rely on scheduling apps to organize volunteers for childcare, youth ministry, hospitality, and other events. Yet availability often goes unconfirmed, and cancellations or no-shows leave pastors and organizers scrambling for last-minute replacements. Group messages ask everyone to pay attention, even when only one person is needed. Repeated notifications can make the next request easier to ignore.

Text Monkey is an AI agent that coordinates volunteers through ordinary text messages. Volunteers need no app, no login, and no additional communication platform. Organizers use a cloud dashboard to review coverage, decisions, and the work that needs their attention.

With built-in Planning Center integration, Text Monkey brings event times and open staffing needs into its coordination workflow. It prepares requests before events and handles replacement searches as cancellations arrive, contacting a selected subset of eligible volunteers instead of messaging the entire group.

Our notification algorithm scores candidates using acceptance rate, average response time, and elapsed time since their last request. Gloo interprets replies and composes personal invitations. Application controls enforce consent, availability, qualifications, serving limits, and human review. Coordinators approve outgoing communications and official record changes; sensitive situations return to a person.

As a church uses Text Monkey, recorded response history informs future recipient selection. Our goal is faster coverage with fewer unnecessary notifications, while preserving the relationships behind volunteering. Interviews with Cornerstone Church coordinators in Boulder informed the problem; time savings remain to be measured. Pastors and organizers can spend less time chasing logistics, while Text Monkey handles the monkey business.
