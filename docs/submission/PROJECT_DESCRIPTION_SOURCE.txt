Churches rely on scheduling apps to organize sign-ups for events that require volunteers to serve in areas such as childcare, youth ministry, and hospitality. However, volunteers are often slow to confirm their availability, if they do so at all. This results in last-minute cancellations and no-shows, which must be handled manually by the pastor or organizer . Group chats/messaging apps have low response rates and send unnecessary notifications, which can result in notification fatigue among volunteers, exacerbating existing communication problems.

Volunteers need no app, no login, and no redundant communication platform. With built-in Planning Center integration, Text Monkey communicates directly with volunteers on an event-by-event basis to fill needed roles and fill cancellations as they arrive in real time. Text Monkey sends texts in advance to fill volunteer roles, notifying only a subset of the volunteer pool. Our optimized notification algorithm assigns each volunteer a score based on their acceptance rate, average response time, and the time elapsed since they were last asked to serve. The longer a church uses Text Monkey, the quicker and more efficiently events are organized, and fewer notifications are sent. Pastors and organizers spend less time managing communication while Text Monkey handles the monkey business.
